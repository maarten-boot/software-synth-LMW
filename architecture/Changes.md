# Changes

Changes between releases.

## Unreleased
- 2026-11-25: Every socket has a ring that shows its kind of signal: solid blue for audio, double orange for control voltages (LFOs, envelopes, key pitch and velocity), dashed magenta for gates; the hover text names it (VCO FM and PM: "also takes audio"), the Schematic has a legend, and Theme ▸ Edit colours has the three colours. Over 9P /ports ends each line with the type.
- 2026-11-25: The tutorial pictures show each lesson's own settings (Lessons 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9), taken by scripts/pictures.sh. Over 9P: /preset chooses a preset as the Presets menus do, /view shows or sets the tab, size and view, /snapshot gives the window as a PNG; lmw9p cat copies binary files unchanged.
- 2026-11-25: Delayed vibrato: LFO 1 and LFO 2 have a Delay fader — after each note starts the LFO fades in (nothing for the first half of the Delay, full at the Delay). Flute, Oboe, Clarinet, Bassoon, Trumpet, French horn, Violin and Male voice (ooo) start their vibrato after 0.4 s. Older patches: Delay 0, as before. While a Delay is set, the Schematic shows it under the LFO and the socket hover texts say where it acts (per note, in the voices) and where not (a cable to OUT or to an LFO Rate).
- 2026-11-25: VCF 1 and VCF 2 Slope has 36 dB: steeper and darker than 24 dB, with a softer resonance (Bandpass and Notch treat it as 24 dB). Saved settings and patches keep their slope. If a DAW project automates Slope, check that lane: the list now starts with 36 dB.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-24)
- 2026-11-24: Patches and saved settings from 1.4.0 load unchanged (the same sound, cables, notes and a panel layout you arranged); the new VCA Velocity starts at 100 %, which is the sound as before. The window now opens filling the screen.
- 2026-11-24: VCA Velocity: a fader on the VCA panel sets how much the note velocity changes the loudness (100 % = as before, 0 % = every note equally loud). The velocity arrives on a new socket, VCA Vel (normally from KEYS Vel), so it shows in the Matrix and on the Schematic and a cable can replace it (e.g. NOISE Rand for random accents). Patches from 1.4.0 play the same; a patch with a cable on VCA Vel loses its cables in 1.4.0 or older.
- 2026-11-24: Cable levels where you patch: right-click a socket (Synth, Effects or Schematic tab) and its menu ends with a fader per connection, output on the left, input on the right; drag to hear the change. Moving a normal connection's fader turns it into cables. Type a level ▸ in the same menu sets one without a mouse.
- 2026-11-24: Inverted modulation: cables into modulation inputs (VCO pitch, FM, PM; VCF cutoff and resonance; FORMANT vowel; VCA CV and Mod; LFO rates) take a level from -100 % to +100 %. Below 0 the source is inverted: an envelope that closes the filter, keys that make low notes louder. A level of 0 keeps the cable. The Matrix slider and cells show the sign. Patches with an inverted cable lose their cables when opened in 1.4.0 or older.
- 2026-11-24: Every fader shows its scale: small ticks beside the track at round values (their spacing shows the curve), the values at the main ticks, and a tag under the name: lin, ± (around 0), log (frequencies and rates) or curve (times and depths: more travel at small values). The hover text says the same, with the value at half way. /info and lmw-mcp report it as "scale".
- 2026-11-24: The window fills the screen and can be resized; the panels keep their size, so there is room to move them (rows can be as wide as the screen). Compact makes the panels smaller and keeps the window. The on-screen keyboard keeps its size, at the right. Lines between the rows show the rack. In a DAW the plugin window can be resized too.
- 2026-11-24: The socket under the mouse, with keyboard focus or under a dragged cable sits on a golden-yellow box with a black edge (Synth, Effects and Schematic tabs). Its colour is in Menu ▸ Theme ▸ Edit colours ("Selected socket"), and in saved themes.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-18)
First release. A semi-modular polyphonic synthesizer (1–32 voices) as standalone app and VST3 plugin for Windows, macOS and Linux: three VCOs, NOISE with sample & hold, mixer, two multimode VCFs (24/12/6 dB; low, high, band, notch), VCA, two ADSRs, two LFOs, reverb; normalled connections that cables (up to 4 per input, with levels) replace, on the Synth tab, the Matrix and the Schematic; patch files and module presets (built-in instrument starting points and your own); notes; black-on-white GUI with adjustable contrast (AA, AAA, Max, custom) and a compact view; a 9P server for remote control (the Kukel GUI); a Windows installer.
